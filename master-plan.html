IRON WEEK
Mobility
10 mins before every session. Targets rotate daily so every restriction gets addressed across the week.
▶Monday — Ankle + Hip
Banded Ankle Distraction
2×45s each
Band around the ankle joint pulling forward, half-kneeling into the wall. The band creates joint space — most effective drill for restricted dorsiflexion. Drive the knee past the toes, keep the heel glued to the floor.
Kneeling Ankle Rocks
3×10 each
Half-kneeling, drive the knee as far past the toes as possible while keeping the heel down. Slow and controlled. You're loading the ankle joint at end range — the key is heel contact throughout.
Cossack Squat
2×8 each
Feet wide, shift your weight to one side into a deep lateral squat. Other leg stays straight. This is the single best drill for lateral hip range — directly transfers to kicking. Go as deep as you can without the heel lifting.
90/90 Hip Transitions
2×10 each
Sit on the floor, both legs at 90 degrees. Rotate both legs to the other side in one smooth movement. Breathe into the stretch at each end. This trains internal and external rotation simultaneously — the foundation of hip mobility.
Hip Airplanes
2×8 each
Stand on one leg, hinge at the hip, rotate the pelvis so the free leg rises and turns skyward. Hold 2 seconds at end range. Trains hip internal rotation under load — critical for kicking mechanics and single leg stability.
Deep Squat Hold
2×60s
Heels elevated on a book or plate, sit into the deepest squat you can hold. Elbows push the knees out, chest up. Each week try to reduce the heel elevation by a few millimetres. This is the long game — consistent daily exposure is what changes this.
▶Tuesday — Shoulder + Neck
Wall Slide
2×10
Back against the wall, arms in W position with elbows and wrists touching the wall. Slide arms up to Y while maintaining contact. This trains the overhead position — if you lose contact with the wall, that's where the restriction is. Go slow.
Thoracic Extension over Foam Roller
2×60s
Place the roller across the mid-back, hands behind the head, let the thoracic spine extend over it. Work up and down the thoracic spine in small movements. This is what's actually blocking your overhead position — not the shoulder joint, the thoracic spine. Non-negotiable.
Lat Stretch in Doorframe
2×45s each
Grab the door frame at shoulder height, step back, sit your hips back and away. You should feel the stretch deep into the lat and armpit. Tight lats are the main reason the bar can't go behind the head — they pull the arms forward.
Shoulder Dislocates (band)
2×10
Wide grip on a band, arms straight, pass the band from front to back overhead and behind. Widen the grip until you can complete the movement without pain. Narrow it slightly each week. This is the most direct drill for overhead shoulder range.
Chin Tucks
3×10 · 5s hold
Standing or sitting tall, draw the chin straight back — like you're trying to make a double chin. Hold 5 seconds. This activates the deep neck flexors that are weak from forward head posture. The muscles that should be holding your head in position have switched off — this wakes them up.
Suboccipital Release
60s
Tennis or anti-stress ball at the base of the skull, lie on it with gentle pressure. Don't roll — just let the weight of your head do the work. The suboccipital muscles are chronically tight in forward head posture and pull the skull forward. Releasing them is the fastest way to feel the difference.
Levator Scapulae Stretch
2×45s each
Ear to shoulder, then look down at a 45-degree angle towards your armpit, gentle pull with the hand. This targets the levator scapulae — the muscle that runs from the top of the shoulder blade to the upper neck and is almost always tight in tech neck.
▶Wednesday — Hip + Lower Back
Adductor Rock Back
2×10 each
Half-kneeling with the non-kneeling leg out to the side, rock the hip into the stretch — shift your weight back and feel the inner thigh lengthen. Direct prep for lateral kicking range. The adductors are almost always the limiting factor for high kicks.
Kneeling Hip Flexor with Rotation
2×45s each
Lunge position, back knee on the floor, drive the hip forward into the stretch then rotate the torso towards the front leg and reach the arm up. Combines hip flexor stretch with thoracic rotation — hits the front of the hip and the spine simultaneously.
Figure 4 / Pigeon Stretch
2×60s each
Lie on your back, cross one ankle over the opposite knee, pull both legs towards your chest. Or pigeon position on the floor — shin parallel to the top of your mat, lean forward. Targets the piriformis and deep hip rotators — the muscles that limit external rotation for round kicks.
Good Mornings
3×10
Feet shoulder-width, hands behind the head or a band across the shoulders, hinge at the hip with a soft knee bend, lower the chest towards the floor, then drive back up through the glutes. Activates the posterior chain — hamstrings, glutes and lower back — before any hinging or squatting work.
Back Extensions
2×12
Lie face down, hands behind the head, lift the chest off the floor by squeezing the glutes and extending the spine. Hold 2 seconds at the top. This counters the flexed posture of sitting and trains the erectors — essential for spine health and posture. Add a band across the shoulders for resistance.
Cossack Squat
2×8 each
Same as Monday. Second exposure this week — lateral hip range needs frequency to change. Go slightly deeper than Monday if possible.
▶Thursday — Ankle + Shoulder
Wall Ankle Stretch — Straight Leg
2×45s each
Stand facing the wall, one foot back, press the heel into the floor and lean into the wall. Straight leg targets the gastrocnemius — the larger calf muscle that crosses the knee. This is a different restriction to the soleus. Both need to be addressed separately.
Wall Ankle Stretch — Bent Knee
2×45s each
Same position but with a bent knee. This takes the gastrocnemius out of the equation and targets the soleus — the deeper calf muscle that limits dorsiflexion most in a squat. The soleus is almost always the culprit when heels lift.
Single Leg Calf Raise — Slow Eccentric
2×12 each
Stand on one leg on a step or edge, raise to full height then lower over 4 seconds to full stretch. This is eccentric loading of the calf — the most effective way to lengthen the muscle tissue over time. Also prevents Achilles issues.
Thread the Needle
2×8 each
On all fours, thread one arm under your body rotating the thoracic spine until the shoulder touches the floor. Follow the hand with your eyes. This rotates the thoracic spine — the segment that's often stuck and limiting both your shoulder and your punching power.
Doorframe Chest Opener
2×45s
Both arms in the door frame at 90 degrees, step one foot through, look slightly up. Stretches the pec minor and opens the anterior shoulder — directly counters the rounded shoulder position from screen time and pressing work.
Shoulder Dislocates (band)
2×10
Second exposure this week. Try narrowing the grip slightly from Tuesday if it felt comfortable.
▶Friday — Full Body + Neck
90/90 Hip Transitions
2×10 each
Third exposure this week. By Friday these should feel easier than Monday — that's the adaptation working.
Banded Ankle Distraction
2×45s each
Second exposure. Compare to Monday — notice if the knee travels further past the toes. That's your progress marker.
Wall Slide
2×10
Second exposure. Focus on keeping the lower back neutral — don't let it arch away from the wall as the arms rise.
Chin Tucks
3×10 · 5s hold
Second exposure this week. Before sparring this is especially important — a strong neutral neck position absorbs punches better and reduces concussion risk.
Neck Isometrics
4 directions × 30s
Hand against the head, resist without moving. Front, back, left, right. Builds the neck musculature that protects against punch impact — non-negotiable for boxing. A strong neck is as important as a strong guard.
Hip Airplanes
2×8 each
Second exposure. Add a 3-second hold at the top if it felt stable on Monday.
Doorframe Pec Stretch
2×45s each
Arm at 90 degrees in the doorframe, step through and rotate away. Before sparring this opens the anterior shoulder and chest — you'll feel your guard position improve immediately after this stretch.

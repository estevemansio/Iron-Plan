Upper Body — Push, Pull, Shoulders, Arms
−
→ 3 sec eccentric on all pressing. Progressive overload — add weight or reps each session. This is your primary muscle building day.
Push
  • KB Floor Press / Vested Deficit Push Ups (alternate weeks)
    Week A: KB Floor Press 4×8–10 — 3 sec eccentric, chest and triceps without shoulder stress. Week B: Vested Deficit Push Ups 4×10–12 — hands on elevated surface, chest below hand level, full range of motion. Add vest weight progressively.
    Chest
  • Incline KB Press / KB Clean & Press (alternate weeks)
    Week A: Incline KB Press 3×10 — upper chest. Week B: KB Clean & Press 3×8 — full body press with explosive clean. Adds variety and power stimulus.
    Chest / Power
Pull
  • Single Arm KB Row / Australian Ring Pull Ups (alternate weeks)
    Week A: Single Arm KB Row 4×10 each side — elbow drives back, squeeze at top. Back thickness. Week B: Australian Ring Pull Ups 4×10–12 — body inclined, pull chest to rings. Natural wrist rotation, mid-back and biceps. More horizontal pull which directly transfers to the pulling mechanics in boxing.
    Back
Shoulders
  • Lateral Raises (KB)
    3×15 — light, strict, slow. Elbow slightly forward. No momentum. Shoulder width and the aesthetic look you want.
    Shoulders
Superset — Biceps + Triceps on Rings
→ Zero rest between bicep and tricep. 60s after both. 3 rounds. Rings give natural wrist rotation — safer and more effective than fixed bar.
  • SS: Ring Curl (Biceps)
    3×10–12 — feet forward, body inclined, curl rings to forehead. Slow eccentric. Full stretch at bottom.
    Biceps
  • SS: Ring Tricep Extension
    3×10–12 — rings at chest height, extend arms fully. Full range with instability. Straight into this after curls.

Option A — Running Rounds (alternate with KB sessions)
−
→ 3 mins on / 1 min off — exact boxing round structure. Weeks 1–4: 6 rounds. Weeks 5–8: 7 rounds. Weeks 9+: 8 rounds. Trains your engine to recover between rounds.
  • 6–8 × 3 min runs / 1 min walk
    Z3–Z4 effort. High but sustainable. Mimics the work-rest ratio of a boxing bout.
1️⃣
Session 1 — AMRAP 20 (2 min rest between rounds)
−
→ Go as heavy as technique allows. The Snatch into Strict Press demands shoulder stability and overhead strength. Each round counts — rest 2 mins and go again until 20 mins is up.
  • 10 Snatch → Strict Shoulder Press
    10 reps each side. Snatch to overhead, lower to rack, strict press. No leg drive on the press — pure shoulder strength.
    Power + Shoulder
2️⃣
Session 2 — 10 Rounds for Time (1 heavy bell)
−
→ Heavy bell, strict press. The combination of clean + press + squat in sequence builds the strength endurance base that keeps you powerful in the later rounds of a fight.
  • Clean → Strict Press
    1 rep — clean from floor, strict press overhead. No momentum.
    Strength
  • 3 Squats
    Racked or goblet. Full depth. 10 rounds for time — log your time each session and beat it.
    Legs
3️⃣
Session 3 — EMOM 30 (add a squat rep every minute)
−
→ When you can't keep up with the minute, take 3 mins full rest and start the rep count again from 1. This session builds mental toughness as much as physical conditioning — exactly what you need in a fight.
  • Clean
    1 rep per minute (constant throughout)
  • Squat
    Start at 1 rep. Add 1 rep every minute until failure. 3 min rest, restart from 1.
    Ladder
  • Thruster
    1 rep per minute (constant)
  • Push Press
    1 rep per minute (constant)
  • Row (KB)
    1 rep per minute (constant)
4️⃣
Session 4 — AMRAP 20 (as heavy as possible)
−
→ Go as heavy as possible while maintaining form. This is your max strength endurance session — the combination of Row, Clean, Lunge and Squat hits every major movement pattern.
  • Row → Clean → Reverse Lunge → Squat
    Continuous sequence. Log rounds completed each session. Beat it next time.
    Full Body
5️⃣
Session 5 — AMRAP 30 (1 bell)
−
→ The Windmill is the most technically demanding movement here — hip mobility, shoulder stability, lateral flexion. Don't rush it. Quality over speed on the Windmill every rep.
  • Clean → Seesaw Lunge → Thruster → Windmill → Push Press
    Continuous flow for 30 mins. Single bell. The Seesaw Lunge alternates legs each rep. The Windmill requires the bell locked overhead — take your time.
    Full Body
6️⃣
Session 6 — 6 Rounds (1 min rest between)
−
→ Descending ladder within each round. The 4-3-2-1 structure means you're always finishing on 1 — no matter how tired you are, one more is always possible. Mental toughness builder.
  • 4 Cleans + 4 Lunges each leg
    Then immediately...
  • 3 Cleans + 3 Lunges each leg
    Then immediately...
  • 2 Cleans + 2 Lunges each leg
    Then immediately...
  • 1 Clean + 1 Lunge each leg
    = 1 round. Rest 1 min. 6 rounds total.
    Ladder
7️⃣
Session 7 — 5 Rounds (2 min rest between)
−
→ Half rack back lunge keeps the KB in rack position on the working side while you lunge backwards. Demands core stability and hip control under load — direct boxing transfer.
  • 5 Cleans → 5 Half Rack Back Lunges → 5 Push Press
    5 rounds. 2 min rest between each. Log your time. Heavy bell — this is a strength endurance session not a cardio one.
    Strength Endurance
8️⃣
Session 8 — 10 Rounds for Time
−
→ Power Clean → Clean Squat → Push Press / Jerk = 1 sequence. Do it 3 times = 1 round. 10 rounds for time. This is the most technically demanding session of the 8 — the transition from Clean Squat into Push Press/Jerk trains the complete power chain from floor to overhead.
  • Power Clean → Clean Squat → Push Press / Jerk
    × 3 = 1 round. 10 rounds for time. Rest as needed between rounds but keep moving. Log total time each session.
    Power
→ Rotation across 8 weeks: Week 1→S1, Week 2→Run, Week 3→S2, Week 4→Run, Week 5→S3, Week 6→Run, Week 7→S4, Week 8→Run. Then repeat with S5–S8. Never do two KB sessions back to back.

Lower Body — Hamstrings, Glutes, Quads, Calves
−
→ Leg Curl alternates with RDL week to week. Both target the posterior chain — Leg Curl is isolated hamstring, RDL is full hamstring and glute. Progressive overload applies to both.
  • Leg Curl (towel + band) / RDL (alternate weeks)
    Week A: Nordic-style Leg Curl 3×8 — feet anchored with band, lower slowly using hamstrings. Incredibly demanding. Week B: KB RDL 4×10 — hinge at hips, feel the stretch, drive through glutes.
    Hamstrings
  • Banded Suitcase Deadlift (KB)
    4×8 each side — KB in one hand, band adds accommodating resistance at lockout. Unilateral posterior chain and anti-lateral flexion core. Direct boxing transfer.
    Posterior Chain
  • Hack Squat (KB)
    4×10 — KB held behind legs, heels slightly elevated. Pure quad focus. 3 sec eccentric. Add weight progressively.
    Quads
  • Summersault Squat (KB)
    3×10 — KB held at horns, elbows inside knees, deep squat. Develops hip mobility and quad depth simultaneously. Go as deep as possible.
    Quads / Mobility
  • Single Leg Calf Raise (slow)
    4×15 each leg — 3 sec up, 3 sec down. Full range, pause at bottom. Boxing footwork lives on your calves and ankles.
    Calves

AM — Speed + Rotational Power · 45 mins max
−
→ No heavy pressing today. Shoulders must be fresh for sparring tonight. This session activates the nervous system without creating fatigue — speed and rotation only.
Speed — Fast Hands
  • Band Punch — Jab + Cross
    4×20 each arm. Light band around wrists, max speed punches. Trains fast twitch fibres in the exact movement pattern. This is where hand speed is built.
    Speed
  • KB Speed Cleans (50% of normal weight)
    4×8 each arm — max velocity, not max weight. Bar should feel like it flies up. You're training speed of movement, not strength.
    Speed
Rotational Power
  • Dead → Rotational Power Clean → Press (KB)
    4×6 each side. KB from floor, rotate and clean explosively, press overhead. Trains the full kinetic chain of a cross — drive from the back foot into rotation into extension. Light-moderate weight today — speed not max load.
    Rotation
  • Explosive Push Ups (light)
    3×8 — hands off floor. Upper body power without shoulder fatigue. Keep it light today.
    Power
Core + Neck
  • Hollow Hold
    3×35s — anti-extension core. Transfers power from legs to fists efficiently.
  • Neck Isometrics (band or manual resistance)
    4 directions × 30s each. Front, back, left, right. Strong neck absorbs punches and reduces concussion risk. 5 mins that matter long term.
    Longevity
🥊
PM — Sparring · Boxing Gym
−
→ Fresh legs, fresh shoulders, nervous system primed but not depleted. Optimal state for sparring. Focus on applying rotation — drive from the back foot on every cross, let the hip lead the hook.
  • Sparring session — gym determined
    Focus: rotation on the cross, hip drive, staying relaxed. Technique over power.

3-Phase Progression
01
Weeks 1–6 · Base Build
Learn gym movements. Power Clean technique dialled in. Wednesday rounds start at 6. Band punch speed work begins. Focus on quality and rotation mechanics over intensity.
02
Weeks 7–12 · Intensity Build
Gym weights significantly heavier. Wednesday rounds up to 8. Band punch speed noticeably faster. Rotational work heavier. Sparring quality improving. Muscle gains visible.
03
Weeks 13+ · Fight Ready
Exhibition fight preparation. Taper 2 weeks before fight. Shift to maintenance calories. 5kg muscle goal achieved. Speed and power at peak. Ready for the ring.

Reglas Clave
Training vs rest day: Usa el toggle. La diferencia principal es el tamaño de porción y la cantidad de carbs.
Post-workout: Shake + plátano después de entrenar. El más importante para ganar músculo. No negociable.
Estreñimiento: Brócoli + espinacas + calabacín diario + cucharadita de semillas de chía en el shake. 3L de agua mínimo los días de entrenamiento.
Magnesio glicinate: 300–400mg antes de dormir. Motilidad intestinal, calidad de sueño y recuperación muscular.
Solo aceite de oliva o mantequilla. Elimina aceites de semillas (girasol, vegetal) completamente. Diferencia medible en inflamación y piel.
Sin frutos secos — alternativas: Pipas de calabaza, semillas de girasol, semillas de chía. El aguacate cubre la mayoría.
Domingo: Proteína alta todo el día, magnesio, 8h de sueño mínimo. El músculo se construye aquí, no entrenando.

FUEL PLAN

Daily — Every Day
Pre-Workout · 5:30am
Bagel + Honey + Banana
1–2 bagels · honey on top · 1 banana on the side · glass of water with 1 tbsp honey + pinch of salt · fast carbs, easy digestion · no fat, no fibre before training
Post-Workout · Immediately After
Protein Shake
30–40g whey protein · water or milk · take within 30 mins of finishing · starts muscle protein synthesis immediately
Breakfast · At the Office
Oats + Greek Yogurt + Honey + Fruit + Dark Chocolate
80g oats · 200g full fat Greek yogurt · 1 tbsp honey · seasonal fruit · 2–3 squares dark chocolate · protein, slow carbs, antioxidants · easy to prep the night before in a Tupperware
